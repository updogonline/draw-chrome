- Monster stat blocks need a [[Chrome]] count and a Chrome keyword on abilities, or Hacking and the [[Bandwidth]] gain trigger have nothing to work with.
- Does the [[Netrunner]] use a Loadout? The Talent uses no Kit.

- Outside the Netrunner slice: [[Stress]] is cleared through downtime projects. Therapy reduces permanent Stress by 1 or temporary Stress by 1d3 per completion, and shorter projects like Party reduce temporary Stress by 1. The Draw Steel project list needs a cyberpunk rewrite, and permanent Stress should become a tracked number gained on install so Therapy can lower it.
