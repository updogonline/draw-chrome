# Stress

Stress is what the life does to you. It runs from 0 to 10, and it is made up of permanent Stress from your [[Chrome]] and temporary Stress from everything else.

The Director decides when you would gain temporary Stress. A gig gone bad, a betrayal or a debt you cannot refuse are all good reasons. Permanent Stress from Chrome is gained automatically.

When you would gain temporary Stress, make a Presence test. Your current Stress makes the test harder:

| Current Stress | Test |
| --- | --- |
| 0 to 3 | No bane |
| 4 to 6 | Bane |
| 7 to 9 | Double bane |

- ≤11: You gain the Stress.
- 12-16: You gain the Stress, or you take a consequence of the Director's choice instead.
- 17+: You do not gain the Stress.

You cannot have more than 10 Stress. While you are at 10 Stress, whenever you would gain Stress, you gain a Cyberpsychosis Complication instead. When you have every Cyberpsychosis Complication, you go cyberpsycho and your character becomes a Director character.
