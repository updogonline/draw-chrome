# Character Creation

You build an edgerunner the same way you build a Draw Steel hero, with a few new names and one new step. Character creation is comprised of these steps:

1. **Background:** Choose where you grew up and who raised you. [[Background]] replaces Culture. Choose an Environment, an Organization and an Upbringing, and gain the skill each one grants (see Background).
2. **Career:** Choose what you did before you started running. You gain your [[Career - Sysadmin|Career]]'s Signature Trait, skills, Wealth and perk, then roll or choose an Inciting Incident (see Career).
3. **Traits:** Spend 3 Trait Points on traits. Your Career and [[Traits]] replace Ancestry (see Traits).
4. **Class:** Choose your class and its subclass. You gain your class's starting characteristics, Stamina, Recoveries and 1st-level features. Netrunners choose a hat as their subclass.
5. **Loadout:** Choose a Loadout, if your class uses one. Netrunners choose a [[Cyberdeck]] and [[ICE]] instead.
6. **Chrome:** Install up to 3 pieces of Starting [[Chrome]] and gain permanent [[Stress]] for any area with 2 or more filled slots (see Chrome). Netrunners also start with a Neural Port.
7. **Complication:** Optionally choose a Complication.
8. **Details:** Name, look, and the reason you have not quit yet.

Unless noted otherwise, each step uses the Draw Steel rules for the step it replaces.
