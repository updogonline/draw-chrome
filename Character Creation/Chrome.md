# Chrome

Chrome is cyberware. It makes you faster, harder and better connected, and every piece of it takes a little of you with it.

Each piece of Chrome fills a slot in one of 5 areas: Optics, Neural Link, Cyberarms, Internal and Cyberlegs. Each area has 3 slots.

Filling slots in an area can give you permanent [[Stress]]:

| Slots Filled in an Area | Permanent Stress |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 0 |
| 2 | 1 |
| 3 | 2 |

For example, a character with 2 Optics slots and 3 Internal slots filled has 3 permanent Stress.

Unless noted otherwise, these rules apply to Chrome:

- Each installed piece counts as 1 Chrome for abilities that count Chrome.
- Chrome is installed or removed during a Crash.
- Removing a piece of Chrome removes any permanent Stress it gave you.

At 1st level, you may install up to 3 pieces of Chrome.

**Netrunners:** You start with a Neural Port installed in a Neural Link slot, and it does not count against your 1st-level Chrome limit. You need it to use Hack abilities, and it means you can be Hacked.

## Starting Chrome

- **Neural Port (Neural Link):** *A socket behind the ear and a cable that goes somewhere you should not.* You can use abilities with the Hack keyword.
- **Cyberoptics (Optics):** *Replacement eyes with more settings than you will ever use.* You ignore concealment created by darkness, smoke or fog.
- **Smartlink (Cyberarms):** *Your gun tells your eyes where to look.* Once per round when you make a ranged weapon strike, it ignores 1 bane from cover.
- **Reflex Booster (Neural Link):** *Your spine fires before your brain has an opinion.* You gain the Reflex Spike triggered action.
- **Subdermal Plating (Internal):** *Armor sheets slid under the skin, visible only when you smile wrong.* You gain a +6 bonus to Stamina, and this bonus increases by 6 at 4th, 7th and 10th levels.
- **Arm Blades (Cyberarms):** *They fold out of your forearms, and they do not fold back in quietly.* Your unarmed free strikes count as weapon strikes and deal an extra 2 damage.
- **Bone Lacing (Internal):** *Your skeleton is threaded with alloy, and the scales stopped making sense years ago.* You gain a +1 bonus to stability.
- **Synthetic Plumbing (Internal):** *Pumps, seals and a coolant that clots faster than blood ever did.* You cannot be made bleeding.
- **Air Gap Module (Neural Link):** *A hardware switch that cuts every wireless port you own.* Hack abilities that target you take a bane.
- **Failsafe (Neural Link):** *A dead-man circuit that power cycles your whole body on command.* If you are Jammed or Tagged, you can use a maneuver to end those conditions on yourself. You can use your Failsafe while you are Jammed.

**Reflex Spike (Triggered Action, Chrome).** Trigger: A creature targets you with a strike. Effect: The strike takes a bane. You can use Reflex Spike once per encounter.
