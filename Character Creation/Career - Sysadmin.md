# Career: Sysadmin

*You kept a corporation's network running for years, and you know exactly where they buried the backdoors.*

**Signature Trait: Old Credentials.** You know how corporate networks are built because you kept one running. You gain an edge on tests to find a way into a corporation's network or facility, and once per Crash you can recall one useful detail about your former employer's security.

- **Skills:** One skill from the crafting group and one skill from the knowledge group.
- **Wealth:** +1.
- **Perk:** One perk from the lore group.

**Inciting Incident.** Something made you quit. Roll a d6 or choose.

| d6 | Inciting Incident |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | You found the file you were not supposed to find. |
| 2 | You were automated out of a job by software you wrote. |
| 3 | You took the blame for a breach you did not cause. |
| 4 | You caused the breach. Nobody knows yet. |
| 5 | Your department was liquidated, along with most of your department. |
| 6 | You let someone in, and they paid better than the company did. |
