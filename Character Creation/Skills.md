# Skills

Draw Chrome keeps Draw Steel's five skill groups, with the lore group renamed to knowledge. Unless noted otherwise, skills work as they do in Draw Steel.

| Group | Skills |
| --- | --- |
| Crafting | Architecture, Chemistry, Cooking, Cybertech, Electronics, Fabrication, Forgery, Gunsmithing, Mechanics, Programming, Tailoring |
| Exploration | Climb, Drive, Endurance, Gymnastics, Heal, Jump, Lift, Navigate, Pilot, Swim |
| Interpersonal | Brag, Empathize, Flirt, Gamble, Interrogate, Intimidate, Lead, Lie, Music, Perform, Persuade, Read Person |
| Intrigue | Alertness, Bypass, Conceal Object, Disguise, Eavesdrop, Escape Artist, Hide, Intrusion, Pick Pocket, Sabotage, Search, Sneak, Track |
| Knowledge | Corporations, Culture, Cybernetics, Ecology, Faith, History, Media, Netlore, Rumors, Society, Strategy, Streets |

These skills are new or work differently from their Draw Steel counterparts:

- **Bypass:** Opening mechanical locks, maglocks and keycard readers.
- **Cybertech:** Installing, repairing and stripping [[Chrome]].
- **Intrusion:** Breaking into networks and secured devices outside of combat. In combat, use Hack abilities.
- **Pilot:** Flying aircraft and drones and steering boats.
- **Programming:** Writing, patching and reverse-engineering software.
