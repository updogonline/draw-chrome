# Traits

You get 3 Trait Points to spend on the following traits. Unless noted otherwise, you can take each trait only once.

**1-Point Traits:**

- **Street Smart:** You gain one skill from the intrigue group.
- **Human Touch:** You gain one skill from the interpersonal group.
- **Well Read:** You gain one skill from the knowledge group.
- **Tinkerer:** You gain one skill from the crafting group.
- **Gut Instinct:** You cannot be surprised.
- **Tough as Nails:** Your Might score is treated as 1 higher for the purposes of resisting potencies, and you gain an edge on Might tests made to resist environmental effects or a creature's traits or abilities.
- **Unaltered:** While you have 1 or fewer [[Chrome]], your maximum Recoveries increase by 1 and the potency of Hack abilities that target you decreases by 1.
- **Good Candidate:** The first time you fill a second slot in an area, that slot gives you no permanent [[Stress]].
- **Bodyguard:** Whenever you or an adjacent creature takes damage from a strike, you can use a triggered action to reduce the damage from the strike by an amount equal to your level.
- **Hold It Together:** As a maneuver, you steady yourself. Until the start of your next turn, you succeed on saving throws on a 4 or higher.
- **Keep Moving:** You gain an edge on tests made using the Endurance skill. When you are slowed, your speed is 3 instead of 2.
- **Spite:** At the start of any combat, you gain 1 surge.
- **Exit Strategy:** You gain a +1 bonus to the distance you can shift when you take the Disengage move action.
- **Roll With It:** Whenever you are force moved, you can use a free triggered action to shift 1 square after the forced movement is resolved.
- **Big:** Your size is 1L. You cannot take Big and Small together.
- **Small:** Your size is 1S. You cannot take Big and Small together.
- **Maker:** When you gain your initial skills, choose two skills from the crafting group, whether you have them or not. Whenever you make a project roll for a crafting project that uses those skills, you gain a +2 bonus to the roll.

**2-Point Traits:**

- **Wired In:** Choose one area: filling its slots never gives you permanent Stress.
- **Staying Power:** Your maximum Recoveries increase by 2.
- **First One's Free!:** Choose one piece of Starting Chrome. You start with it installed, it does not count against your 1st-level Chrome limit, and it gives you no permanent Stress.
- **Grit:** If you are frightened, slowed or weakened, you can use a maneuver to end one of those conditions.
- **Nothing Left to Lose:** You cannot be made frightened.
