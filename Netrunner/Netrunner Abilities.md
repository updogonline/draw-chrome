# Netrunner Abilities

Hack abilities follow the rules in [[Netrunning]]. [[Conditions|Overclocked]] effects apply while your [[Bandwidth]] is negative.

## Signature Ability

### //RUN.ZAP

*Hack, Ranged, Strike · Main action · Ranged 10 · One creature*

A simple daemon that zaps a hostile's systems.

Power Roll + Reason:

- ≤11: 2 + R lightning damage.
- 12-16: 4 + R lightning damage.
- 17+: 6 + R lightning damage.

Overclocked: The target takes an extra 2 lightning damage. You take 2 damage that cannot be reduced.

## Bandwidth Abilities

At 1st level, choose one 3-Bandwidth ability and one 5-Bandwidth ability.

### 3-Bandwidth Abilities

#### //PING.ECHO (3 Bandwidth)

*Area, Hack · Main action · 3 burst · Each enemy in the area that can be Hacked*

Ping echoes through the halls of a creature's chrome zapping their uninsulated wires.

Power Roll + Reason:

- ≤11: 2 lightning damage.
- 12-16: 4 lightning damage; R < Average, slowed (save ends).
- 17+: 6 lightning damage; R < Strong, slowed (save ends).

Overclocked: The burst increases by 2. You take 1d6 damage.

#### //SCRUB.LOGS (3 Bandwidth)

*Ranged · Maneuver · Ranged 10 · Self or one ally*

When you Ghost Signal, you scrub an ally from every feed in the area.

Effect: Until the start of your next turn, creatures with [[Chrome]] take a bane on strikes against the target.

Overclocked: The bane becomes a double bane. You are slowed until the end of your next turn.

#### //BREACH.PROTOCOL (3 Bandwidth)

*Hack, Ranged, Strike · Main action · Ranged 10 · One creature*

When you Override, you take control of the target's chrome limbs for a moment.

Power Roll + Reason:

- ≤11: 3 + R lightning damage.
- 12-16: 5 + R lightning damage; the target makes a free strike against a creature of your choice within its reach.
- 17+: 8 + R lightning damage; the target makes a free strike against a creature of your choice within its reach; R < Strong, weakened (save ends).

Overclocked: You slide the target up to a number of squares equal to your Reason score before any free strike. You take 1d6 damage.

#### //PERIPHERAL.OVERRIDE (3 Bandwidth)

*Hack, Ranged, Strike · Main action · Ranged 10 · One creature*

For half a second the target's cyberlegs belong to you, and you walk them somewhere stupid.

Power Roll + Reason:

- ≤11: 2 + R lightning damage; slide 2 + R.
- 12-16: 4 + R lightning damage; slide 4 + R.
- 17+: 6 + R lightning damage; slide 6 + R, and the target falls prone.

Overclocked: You can slide the target vertically. You take 1d6 damage.

### 5-Bandwidth Abilities

#### //RBAC.DAEMON (5 Bandwidth)

*Hack, Ranged, Strike · Main action · Ranged 10 · One creature*

When you Feedback Loop, you turn the target's own implants against it.

Power Roll + Reason:

- ≤11: 6 + R lightning damage.
- 12-16: 9 + R lightning damage.
- 17+: 13 + R lightning damage.

Effect: The target takes an extra 1 damage for each Chrome it has, to a maximum of 5.

Overclocked: Each other enemy within 3 squares of the target that can be Hacked takes lightning damage equal to your Reason score. You take 1d6 damage.

#### //RUN.PUPPET (5 Bandwidth)

*Hack, Ranged, Strike · Main action · Ranged 10 · One creature*

Every servo in the target's body answers to you now.

Power Roll + Reason:

- ≤11: 4 + R lightning damage; slide 3.
- 12-16: 6 + R lightning damage; slide 5.
- 17+: 9 + R lightning damage; slide 7, and the target falls prone.

Overclocked: The target makes a free strike against each creature it ends the slide adjacent to. You take 1d6 damage.

#### //EXEC.REPLAY (5 Bandwidth)

*Hack · Maneuver · Ranged 10 · Self or one ally with Chrome*

You pull the last six seconds from their reflex buffer and run them again.

Effect: The target uses an ability with a base Heroic Resource cost of 7 or lower that they have already used this round, without paying the base cost.

Overclocked: You take 1d6 damage and are slowed (save ends).

#### //PATCH.FIREWALL (5 Bandwidth)

*Hack · Maneuver · Ranged 10 · Self or one ally with Chrome*

Every port on their body slams shut, and every hostile packet bounces.

Effect: The target gains 10 temporary Stamina and 2 surges. While they have temporary Stamina from this ability, Hack abilities that target them take a bane.

Overclocked: You cannot use maneuvers (save ends).
