# Netrunner Tradition

[[Netrunner]] abilities are grouped into categories according to their strengths

- **Black Hat:** You break people. Your abilities attack the [[Chrome]] inside your enemies, overriding limbs, scrambling optics and frying neuralware.
- **Grey Hat:** You own the hardware. Your abilities hijack your enemies' Chrome, dragging them around by their own cyberarms and tripping them with their own cyberlegs.
- **White Hat:** You keep the crew alive. Your abilities run your team's network, boosting allies' reflexware and lagging enemies by throttling their connection.

Choose a hat. Your hat grants 1 feature and 1 triggered action.

## Black Hat

- **Zero Day:** The first time each encounter you use a Hack ability against a target, the power roll gains an edge.
- **Backtrace (Triggered Action).** Trigger: A creature within 10 squares targets you or an ally with an ability that has the Chrome keyword. Effect: The creature takes lightning damage equal to your Reason score.

## Grey Hat

- **Puppet Strings:** Whenever you force move a creature, you can move it 1 additional square.
- **Trip Wire (Triggered Action).** Trigger: An enemy with Chrome willingly moves adjacent to you or an ally. Effect: The enemy's cyberlegs lock up. It ends its movement and falls prone.

## White Hat

- **Low Latency:** Each ally within 10 squares of you gains a +1 bonus to speed.
- **Packet Loss (Triggered Action).** Trigger: An enemy within 10 squares makes a strike against an ally. Effect: The strike takes a bane.
