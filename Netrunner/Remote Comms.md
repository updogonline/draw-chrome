# Remote Comms

You know the Machine Code language. Additionally, you can remotely communicate with any creatures with at least 1 [[Chrome]] within distance of your //RUN.ZAP ability if they share a language with you and you know of each other. When you communicate with someone this way, they can respond the same way.
