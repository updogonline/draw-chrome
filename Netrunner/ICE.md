# ICE

Most netrunners create their own defensive layers of programming and electronics known as ICE. You can change your ICE during a Crash.

- **Tar Pit ICE:** Your ICE slows the actuators in your enemies. Whenever a creature deals damage to you, their speed is reduced by an amount equal to your Reason score and they can't use triggered actions until the end of their next turn.
- **Kickback ICE:** Your ICE causes the chrome of your enemies to act against their will. Whenever an adjacent creature deals damage to you, you can use a free triggered action to push that creature up to a number of squares equal to your Reason score.
- **Firewall ICE:** Your ICE reacts to dangers and protects you from harm. Whenever you take damage, after the damage resolves, you gain damage immunity equal to your Reason score until the end of your turn.
- **Spoof ICE:** Your ICE removes your last known location from enemy sensors. Whenever you take damage, you become invisible until the end of your next turn.
