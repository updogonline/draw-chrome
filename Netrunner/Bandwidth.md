# Bandwidth

Bandwidth is how much processing your rig can push before the feedback starts cooking your brain. It is the [[Netrunner]]'s Heroic Resource.

At the start of combat, you gain Bandwidth equal to your Streak. At the start of each of your turns, you gain 1d3 Bandwidth.

The first time each combat round a creature within 10 squares of you uses an ability with the [[Chrome]] keyword, you gain 1 Bandwidth.

Unless noted otherwise, you may spend Bandwidth you do not have. Your Bandwidth can go negative, to a minimum of:

-(1 + your Reason score)

While your Bandwidth is negative, you are [[Conditions|Overclocked]]. At the end of each of your turns while Overclocked, you take 1 damage for each point of negative Bandwidth. This damage cannot be reduced.

For example, a Netrunner with Reason 2 can go down to -3 Bandwidth. At -3, they take 3 damage at the end of each of their turns.

When combat ends, your Bandwidth resets to 0. If you end combat at your maximum negative Bandwidth, you lose 1 Recovery and your nose bleeds as it usually does.
