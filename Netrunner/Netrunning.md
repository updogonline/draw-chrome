# Netrunning

Outside of combat, you slip into a location's network and work the problem from inside, so your crew does not have to.

**Netrun Assist:** You can assist any power roll, including your own, without rolling. Treat your Netrun Assist as a 12-16 result. You can do this once, and you regain the use whenever your crew gains Streak. A Netrun Assist is separate from the regular assist, and you can still assist normally.

You can make another Netrun Assist before your crew gains Streak, but you must roll it. Your first extra Netrun Assist has no edge or bane, your second has a bane and every Netrun Assist after that has a double bane. Each extra Netrun Assist gives the Director 1 Heat.

When your crew gains Streak, your extra Netrun Assists reset.

A creature can be Hacked if it has at least 1 [[Chrome]].

Abilities with the Hack keyword can only target creatures that can be Hacked. Hack abilities ignore cover but not concealment.

A Purist with no Chrome cannot be Hacked. That is the point of being a Purist.
