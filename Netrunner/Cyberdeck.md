# Cyberdeck

Your cyberdeck is the hardware you jack into, and it shapes how you run. Choose one of the following Cyberdecks. You can swap your Cyberdeck during a Crash.

- **Combat Deck:** You can wear light armor and wield light weapons effectively, even though you do not have a Loadout. You can use light armor and light weapon gear. If you have a Loadout, you cannot take this Cyberdeck.
- **Hardened Deck:** You gain a +6 bonus to Stamina, and this bonus increases by 6 at 4th, 7th and 10th levels. You also gain a +1 bonus to stability.
- **Recon Deck:** Your ranged [[Netrunner]] abilities gain a +2 bonus to distance.
- **Covert Deck:** Your damage-dealing Netrunner abilities gain a +1 bonus to rolled damage.
- **Quick Deck:** You gain a +1 bonus to speed and to the distance you can shift when you take the Disengage move action.
