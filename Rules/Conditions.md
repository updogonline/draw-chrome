# Conditions

Unless noted otherwise, Draw Steel's conditions work as written: bleeding, dazed, frightened, grabbed, prone, restrained, slowed, taunted and weakened.

Draw Chrome adds these conditions:

- **Jammed:** You cannot use abilities with the Hack or [[Chrome]] keywords, [[Remote Comms]] or any other action that relies on your Chrome or your gear's software.
- **Tagged:** A targeting system has your signature. Strikes against you ignore concealment, and you cannot be hidden.
- **Overclocked:** Your [[Bandwidth]] is negative (see Bandwidth).

Jammed and Tagged usually come from EMP grenades, Faraday nets, Smartlinks and spotters. A character with a Failsafe can end them on themselves.
