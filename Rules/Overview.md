# Overview

Draw Chrome is a cyberpunk hack of Draw Steel. You play edgerunners at the top of their game, and the fight gets hotter the longer it runs.

Unless noted otherwise, use the Draw Steel rules as written. These rules carry over without changes:

- Power Rolls (2d10 + characteristic, 3 tiers), Edges, Banes and Surges.
- Alternating initiative.
- Negotiation (Interest and Patience). Every meeting with a Fixer is a Negotiation.
- Montage Tests. Use them for heists, infiltrations, chases and getting out of the district before the corp response team arrives.
